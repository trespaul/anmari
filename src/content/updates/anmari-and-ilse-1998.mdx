Anmari and Ilse's first album, released in 1998, features the world premiere of Roelof Temmingh's "Meanwhile" and the first recording of Wellesz's Sonata (Op. 31), together with Rachmaninov's Sonata in g minor (Op. 19), Fauré's Elégie in C minor (Op. 24), and Ravel's "Habañera".
