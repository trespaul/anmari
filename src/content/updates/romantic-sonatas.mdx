*Romantic Sonatas* (2015) is the second album Anmari van der Westhuizen has recorded with pianist Ilse Schumann, the first being [*Rachmaninov, Wellesz, Temmingh, Fauré, & Ravel* (1998)](/post/anmari-and-ilse-1998). The album comprises sonatas by Chopin and Grieg, presented alongside arrangements of three short pieces by Fritz Kreisler.
